# Curated Source Manifest

## Purpose

This manifest records the material copied into the project before drafting the MSME series. The copies provide a stable research baseline. They are not all equally authoritative, and inclusion does not imply endorsement.

The source library contains **60 files**:

| Collection | Files | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| `00_anchors` | 3 | Governing article, rough research notebook and semantic comparison |
| `01_kba_research_package` | 29 | KBA-oriented proposals, specification, public-data baseline, data dictionary, correspondence, reproducibility scripts and captured aggregate data |
| `02_credit_risk_research` | 11 | Bayesian, temporal, implementation, publication and credit-risk enhancement research |
| `03_kesonia_enterprise_and_funding` | 10 | KESONIA, pricing, IFRS 9, ALM, FTP, risk-neutral valuation, lakehouse and RegTech research |
| `04_structural_comparators` | 7 | Frozen copies of Mwendo Pamoja Parts 1, 2a, 2b, 3, 4, 5 and 6 |

Temporary Office lock files, Python bytecode caches, generated PDFs and unrelated publication material were excluded.

## Source-handling rules

1. Do not edit files under `source_library`.
2. Cite the underlying authoritative source, not a research note that merely links to it.
3. Recheck current legal, regulatory, benchmark and institutional claims at the time each part is drafted.
4. Record inaccessible, superseded or contradictory sources rather than silently discarding them.
5. Distinguish observed data, source-backed facts, derived quantities, hypotheses, modelling choices and recommendations.
6. Preserve public-data observation dates and never combine flows and stocks as if they share a denominator.
7. Treat copied source code as research material until it passes a separate reproducibility and security review.
