## Prior-role rule

The current baseline specification in A2.5 uses Gaussian fixed-effect coefficients and half-Student-t(3) priors on positive sector, region and vintage standard deviations. These are different parameters and different priors:

- A Student-t coefficient prior is on a signed regression coefficient, for example βⱼ ~ Student-t₃(0, sⱼ). Its normal–Gamma representation conditions on a coefficient-specific precision λⱼ.
- A half-Student-t prior is on a positive group standard deviation σₖ > 0. It controls the amount of pooling for Gaussian group effects uⱼ | σₖ ~ Normal(0, σₖ²), and its auxiliary inverse-Gamma representation updates the scale in the centered parameterisation.
- The common value ν = 3 means three degrees of freedom. In the normal–Gamma mixture, the Gamma shape and rate are both 3/2. This does not make σₖ a regression coefficient or make uⱼ marginally identical to a Student-t coefficient.
- A Student-t coefficient prior is an explicitly declared alternative/sensitivity model unless the registered model specification is changed. Do not imply it is active merely because a half-t prior uses ν = 3. Record the exact coefficient-prior family, scales, group-scale prior and parameterisation independently.

Pólya–Gamma augmentation supplies the conditional Gaussian likelihood block for a logistic model. Gaussian coefficient priors give a fixed prior precision; a Student-t coefficient prior gives a Gaussian block conditional on its latent λⱼ values. The full marginal posterior remains a mixture and the article must describe which version is fitted.
