Underwrite for Collection: a completed research whitepaper

How can lenders support sustainable MSME repayment across a facility’s life?

What began as LinkedIn articles has become six manuscripts across five parts, with two reproducible cases. The paper connects product design, cash-flow evidence, underwriting, fair servicing, monitoring, collection and recovery to expected loss, capital and funding.

Financing an operating business starts with its cash generation, obligations and continuing activity. Financing ring-fenced receivables follows identified repayment claims and the cash dedicated to them. The cases connect both: the enterprise’s operating cycle supports loan repayment; transferred loan claims then feed vehicle accounts, reserves and a distribution waterfall.

Exploring the IFoA introduction to banking module supplied an academic motivation and an actuarial lens for these practical questions.

Judy Chege’s posts and articles were a major influence on the credit-lifecycle framing. Sukrit Ghildiyal’s comment on Geofrey Osiemo’s SVM/IFRS 9 post prompted the second article and its cash-provenance analysis.

CPA James K. and Felix Sifuna’s NPL discussion helped shape the first article and Part 1. The chapter now distinguishes dates, populations and denominators, and places aggregate ratios alongside the lifecycle cash, cohort, cure and recovery measures the research question requires.

Beata Lubinska’s Treasury, ALM and FTP discussions, and Mariusz Podsiadlo’s deep-learning ALM prototype, also helped develop the questions explored here.

A follow-on five-article series explores credit experiments, sequential collection, reject inference, Treasury control and bank-controlled agentic AI with meaningful human review.

The whitepaper offers a research foundation, with worked cases, methods, governance and questions for empirical validation.
