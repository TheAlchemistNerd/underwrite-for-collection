# Part 1 Detailed Outline

## Title and thesis

**Underwrite for Collection: The MSME Credit Lifecycle and Collection Cascades**

Thesis: A loan is successfully underwritten when the product, evidence, monitoring and intervention design support sustainable contractual or restructured cash flows after disbursement.

## Planned structure

### Abstract

State the problem, Kenyan setting, lifecycle proposition, ecosystem scope and contribution of the six-part series.

### 1. When a profitable month still misses Tuesday's instalment

- Open with an illustrative MSME operating cycle.
- Separate profit from cash timing and borrower from facility risk.
- Define the population using CBK's survey framework and note institutional segmentation.
- Establish the cash conversion cycle as the narrative unit.
- Figure 1: operating cash clocks and the repayment calendar.

### 2. Read the portfolio through matched populations

- Present CBK December 2024 MSME account and value results.
- Show how micro accounts and medium-enterprise balances create different count and value pictures.
- Present KBA June 2026 selected dashboard view separately.
- Present June 2026 full banking-sector NPL movement separately.
- Explain vintage, stock, flow, account and value denominators.
- Table 1: a denominator discipline for management reporting.

### 3. Collection begins in product architecture

- Build on Berger and Udell's lending-technology concept.
- Describe product purpose, tenor, repayment frequency, grace, limit, security, price and monitoring.
- Define sustainable collectability.
- Show the conflict created by volume-only incentives.
- Connect Basel granting, administration, monitoring and early remediation.
- Figure 2: constructive lifecycle loop.

### 4. From cash-flow pressure to a collection cascade

- Trace external shock, operational response, liquidity pressure, arrears, supplier and tax effects, limit utilisation, restructuring and default.
- Add feedback loops and intervention windows.
- Distinguish cure, durable cure, re-default and recovery.
- Figure 3: MSME credit and collection cascade with stabilising branches.

### 5. One borrower, many institutions, one economic outcome

- Map the roles of MSME, bank, MFB, DCP where relevant, bureau, payments, accountants, platforms, KBA, regulators, DFI/guarantee providers and service firms.
- Explain information and incentive discontinuities.
- Define proposed collaboration without implying commitment.
- Figure 4: ecosystem responsibility and evidence loop.

### 6. From a strong proposition to testable evidence

- Introduce the limits of retrospective association.
- Motivate randomised pilots and quasi-experiments.
- Use the microfinance experiment as a methodological illustration of heterogeneous outcomes.
- State the bank-controlled model-to-data path.
- Map Parts 2a–6 and their distinct questions.

### Conclusion

Return to the opening MSME and restate collection as a design property measured through sustainable, risk-adjusted cash and fair customer outcomes.
