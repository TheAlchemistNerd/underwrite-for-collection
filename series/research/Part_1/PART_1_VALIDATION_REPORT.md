## Interpretation controls

- The CBK December 2024 MSME results, the CBK full-sector annual results, the selected KBA MSME dashboard and the CBK June 2026 full-sector survey are not treated as one continuous time series.
- NPL stock, gross-loan denominator, account count, value share and realised recovery cash remain distinct measures.
- The 2024–26 aggregates motivate the paper's research question about sustainable repayment and realised recovery. They do not measure cohort-level cure, re-default or net workout recovery and do not identify a causal effect of lending or collection practices.
- The article's reporting tone is descriptive. LinkedIn posts and comments supplied discussion prompts; they are not used as evidence for official industry statistics.
