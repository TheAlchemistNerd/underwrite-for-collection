## Statistical interpretation rules

- Keep the December 2024 CBK MSME survey, December 2024–December 2025 CBK full-sector annual series, the selected June 2026 KBA MSME dashboard and the March–June 2026 CBK full-sector survey distinct.
- Show dates, portfolio population, numerator, denominator and value/count basis beside each ratio. A falling ratio can coexist with a nearly unchanged NPL stock when the performing loan denominator grows.
- Use the aggregates to motivate the research question: what evidence explains sustainable repayment and realised recovery through the credit lifecycle? Do not present unlike populations as a continuous trend or interpret a ratio change as proof that a policy caused it.
- Label bank-reported expectations as expectations. Distinguish NPL stock from realised recovery cash, cohort performance, cure, re-default and net recovery after workout costs.

## Excluded opening claims

- Do not compare first-half MSME originations directly with full-sector NPL stocks as if they were matched performance measures.
- Do not infer that an observed collection contact caused repayment.
- Do not describe all MSMEs as informationally opaque or all transaction data as reliable.
- Do not claim that aggressive collection improves asset quality.
- Do not imply institutional participation without written confirmation.
