## Claims approved for the manuscript

| Claim ID | Proposed claim | Classification | Source | Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1-C01 | MSMEs differ materially in scale, sector, legal form, cash conversion and information availability. | Definition and economic proposition | [1], [2], [5] | Bank segmentation must be stated because statutory and operational definitions may differ. |

| P1-C03 | Micro enterprises represented most accounts, while medium enterprises represented most value in commercial banks. | Observed survey result | [1] | Count and value distributions answer different questions. |
| P1-C04 | The 2024 survey reported 252,502 non-performing MSME accounts worth KSh149.8 billion, equal to 28.4 percent by account count and 19.1 percent by value. | Observed survey result | [1] | These are December 2024 survey measures, not June 2026 KBA dashboard measures. |
| P1-C05 | KBA's June 2026 selected view reported a 24.5 percent value-based NPL ratio and KSh234.67 billion of NPL outstanding. | Observed dashboard result | [3] | Retain filters, population, date and value basis. Do not splice it into the CBK 2024 denominator. |
| P1-C06 | The banking-sector gross NPL ratio improved from 15.6 percent in March 2026 to 14.8 percent in June 2026 as gross loans rose and gross NPLs declined. | Observed sector result | [4] | Full banking-sector population, not an MSME-only series. |

## Excluded opening claims

- The reported KSh245.1 billion of first-half 2026 MSME originations will not be placed beside a full-sector NPL stock as a performance comparison.
- The series will not infer that an observed collection contact caused repayment.
- The series will not describe all MSMEs as informationally opaque or all transaction data as reliable.
- The series will not imply that aggressive collection improves asset quality.
- The series will not announce participation by KBA, CBK, a bank, DFI or other institution before written confirmation.
