## 5. Reuse, ownership and the limits of this case

- Hierarchical logistic modelling, P-splines, shrinkage, residual representations, default timing, multi-state cure/recovery and dependence are already developed in EA and the Part 2a/2b research packages. The current deterministic case consumes assumed values; it does not fit those models. Data feasibility, point-in-time validation, held-out time periods and intervention identification remain genuine empirical work.
- Legal applicability, contract permissions and reporting policies retain their named institutional owners. A teaching example cannot establish Kenyan applicability or approved ECL/capital treatment merely by referencing a prior source ledger.
- RegTech-KESONIA owns bank Treasury curves, benchmark operations, ALM/FTP implementation and institutional integration. FRTB, SA-CCR and regulatory CVA are separately scoped bank/trading/derivative topics, not missing requirements for this MSME order case.
- Mwendo owns its distinct driver/platform/insurer/receivables structure, permitted premium/refund flows, local currency obligations and controlled financing term sheet. Its reserve or capital choices must not be imported into the independent MSME example.
- SokoIntel may consume the reconciled cases through X3's separately proposed credit vertical. A specification proposal or copied review note is not product implementation. Investor valuation, business repayment capacity and ring-fenced receivables analysis remain separate views.
- IFRS 17 and insurance-accounting work retains its reporting-entity/contract boundary. Bayesian relativities supplies methods and reuse opportunities; it does not validate an MSME PD, claim distribution or guarantee.
- Owner earnings/SDE and borrower CFADS are different quantities. An owner's draw or new loan is not operating revenue, and an investment valuation measure cannot replace contractual repayment capacity without a reconciliation.
- Public Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac data can support specified mortgage lifecycle exercises. It cannot supply missing Kenyan MSME cash-provenance, collection-treatment, legal re-verification, guarantee or operating-cost evidence by relabelling variables.
