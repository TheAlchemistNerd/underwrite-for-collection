# Recommended publication route

Do not submit the complete 165-page compilation directly to a conventional journal. Retain it as the programme-level white paper, then extract focused manuscripts.

1. **Kenya Bankers Association**

   Best immediate institutional audience. Send the compilation privately to `research@kba.co.ke` and request off-cycle Working Paper consideration, an Economic Bulletin adaptation, or guidance for the next Banking Research Conference. KBA’s Working Paper Series is intended to stimulate banking-industry and policy discussion. [KBA Working Paper Series](https://www.kba.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Working_Paper_WPS_01_123.pdf)
2. **Digital Finance, Springer Nature**

   Best peer-reviewed home for the overall lifecycle-credit architecture after condensing Parts 2a, 2b and 4 and adding an empirical or controlled synthetic evaluation. Its scope expressly includes financial-data analytics, theoretical modelling, financial inclusion, technology implementation, regulation, microfinance and loan products. It is a hybrid journal and accepts Word or LaTeX submissions. [Aims and scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/42521/aims-and-scope), [submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/42521/submission-guidelines)
3. **The Journal of Risk Finance**

   Best match for a concentrated Part 5 paper covering KESONIA, ECL, economic capital, ALM, FTP, guarantees and ring-fenced receivables finance. It welcomes conceptual, technical and empirical work on liability management, securitisation, financial risk, FinTech, Insurtech and RegTech. Articles must be 3,000–8,000 words, including references and appendices; Word is required, although a PDF may accompany it. [Journal and author guidelines](https://www.emeraldgrouppublishing.com/journal/jrf)
4. **The Journal of Credit Risk**

   Best home for the HLR, lifecycle-state, cure, recovery, dependence and economic-capital methodology. It covers credit scoring, machine learning, default estimation, copulas, recoveries, liquidity-credit interaction, securitisation and capital adequacy. Research papers are limited to 8,000 words including references and initially require an anonymised PDF. [Scope](https://www.risk.net/journal-of-credit-risk), [submission requirements](https://www.risk.net/static/risk-journals-submission-guidelines)
5. **Financial Innovation**

   Strong option once the AI and alternative-data architecture has an empirical demonstration. The journal welcomes theoretical, empirical and practical research involving AI, big data, IoT and financial innovation. It is fully open access; its currently listed APC is £1,190, US$1,750 or €1,440, with waiver requests made at submission. [Scope and submission details](https://link.springer.com/journal/40854/submission-guidelines)
6. **Journal of Financial Regulation and Compliance**

   Appropriate for a paper extracted from Parts 3 and 4 on regulatory orchestration, fair intervention, model governance and bank-controlled research sandboxes. It explicitly operates at the intersection of academic research and regulatory practice. [Journal page](https://www.emerald.com/jfrc)
7. **Financial Analysts Journal**

   A stretch but credible destination for a narrowly investor-focused adaptation of Part 5 addressing KESONIA-linked assets, structured-credit cash flows, liquidity, risk transfer and investor outcomes. The full institutional architecture is too broad for this outlet. [CFA Institute submission page](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research/financial-analysts-journal/submit-to-financial-analysts-journal)

## Best sequence

My recommendation is:

1. Approach KBA with the complete white paper.
2. Extract the Part 5 paper for *The Journal of Risk Finance*.
3. Develop the HLR and lifecycle-state model into an empirical paper for *The Journal of Credit Risk*.
4. Combine the data, modelling and sandbox architecture into an evaluated paper for *Digital Finance*.
5. Use SSRN only after selecting the journals and checking their preprint policies. SSRN provides a natural finance working-paper channel, but premature publication of the entire compilation could complicate originality and overlap declarations. [SSRN submission portal](https://papers.ssrn.com/)
