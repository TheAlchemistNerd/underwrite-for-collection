## Claims deliberately excluded or narrowed

- No official CBK regulatory-sandbox duration or automatic route is asserted. The manuscript uses a bank-controlled research environment and a regulator-engaged controlled pilot under the pathway agreed for the participating institutions.
- Section 35 is not described as banning every automated model or requiring human approval of every low-impact output. The analysis is tied to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
- The four-fifths rule is not treated as Kenyan law or a fairness safe harbour.
- Equalized odds is not the sole fairness objective.
- A reminder, grace period or restructure is not assumed to improve repayment. It is an intervention whose cash, business and customer effects must be estimated.
- A behavioural pattern is not labelled as unwillingness, deception, distress or intent without independently governed evidence.
- A PSI value is not a legal breach. Drift metrics initiate investigation under an internal model-governance policy.
