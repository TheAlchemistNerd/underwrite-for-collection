# Part 3 Detailed Outline

## Working title

**Regulatory Orchestration, Fair Intervention and Collection Governance**

## Governing question

How can a lender convert early risk evidence into timely and proportionate action while preserving privacy, affordability, explanation, human judgement, contestability, fair treatment and durable business continuity?

## Narrative and analytical sequence

1. **From warning to permission.** Begin with an MSME whose cash receipts and instalment date are temporarily misaligned. Show why an elevated posterior probability is evidence for review, not authority to alter a contract or intensify collection.
2. **Four decision objects.** Separate prognostic inference, causal treatment effect, policy eligibility and the customer-facing action. Define the intervention contract and the lifecycle outcome ledger.
3. **A graduated intervention library.** Move from information and payment alignment through temporary relief, modification, restructuring and recovery. Assign an owner, eligibility, exclusions, cost bearer, stop rule and complaint route to every action.
4. **The Credit Policy and Compliance Gate.** Formalise the feasible action set as the intersection of legal, contractual, conduct, affordability, operational and portfolio constraints. Carry posterior uncertainty and data quality into deferral and human review.
5. **Kenya-first control architecture.** Apply the Data Protection Act, Data Protection (General) Regulations, DCP Regulations, CRB Regulations, Consumer Protection Act and CBK Prudential Guideline on Consumer Protection according to entity and activity scope.
6. **Explanation, fairness and customer outcomes.** Build explanations from explicit evidence and policy reason codes. Measure calibration, error, access, contact burden, intervention receipt, complaints, durable cure, re-default and net recovery across relevant groups.
7. **Experimental governance.** Randomise only among permissible and genuinely uncertain alternatives after mandatory protections. Define estimands, logged propensities, doubly robust evaluation, stopping rules and quasi-experimental alternatives.
8. **Portfolio escalation and management information.** Aggregate individual decisions into portfolio triggers without turning statistical heuristics into legal rules. Link escalation to investigation, review, controlled restriction and documented release.
9. **Conclusion.** Present regulatory orchestration as the mechanism that lets continuous underwriting support sustainable collection without collapsing prediction, policy and treatment into one automated act.

## Mathematical and computational content

- Feasible action-set intersection across legal and operating controls.
- Constrained expected-value decision rule with cash, customer-harm and operating-cost terms.
- Posterior action-deferral rule for uncertain or out-of-support cases.
- Group-conditional calibration and outcome-gap measures with interval estimates.
- Durable-cure and net-recovery estimands.
- Doubly robust off-policy value estimator for a logged intervention policy.
- Posterior portfolio-breach probability and escalation logic.
