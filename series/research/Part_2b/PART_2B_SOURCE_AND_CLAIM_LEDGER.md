# Part 2b Source and Claim Ledger

## Purpose

This ledger fixes the evidence base before Part 2b prose is drafted. The final manuscript uses an independent IEEE sequence ordered by first appearance. A source supports only the proposition recorded here. International research supplies statistical methods and comparative evidence; Kenyan regulatory propositions remain anchored to Kenyan primary material.

## Citation and language controls
