# Word-Count and Quality Gates

## Count basis

The series uses two counts.

**Full-source count** includes headings, body prose, table text, mathematical exposition, captions, Mermaid labels, appendices and fenced code. It excludes the References section and any glossary.

**Editorial-prose count** excludes fenced code and Mermaid source while retaining headings, prose, tables, captions, mathematics and appendices. It excludes the References section and any glossary.

Where an appendix follows the References section in a working source, the counting tool must identify the appendix structurally and retain it. The preferred final order is body, appendices, then References, which removes this ambiguity.

## Draft bands

- Outline: no word requirement.
- First complete draft: target ±5 percent.
- Technical-review draft: target ±2 percent.
- Publication candidate: full-source target ±1 percent and editorial prose at or above the stated target.

No part may meet its count through repeated definitions, inflated captions, duplicated caveats, decorative code or a reference section misclassified as prose.

## Quality gates

Each publication candidate must pass:

- narrative continuity from MSME experience to mechanism and evidence;
- stable notation and defined units;
- internally reconciled quantities and horizons;
- prediction–causation–policy separation;
- primary-source regulatory verification;
- point-in-time and leakage review;
- posterior predictive and causal-identification review as applicable;
- conduct, privacy and human-review analysis;
- IEEE citation sequencing and reference completeness;
- equation, diagram and table rendering;
- removal of unsupported guarantees and invented institutional commitments;
- constructive language that advances the architecture.
