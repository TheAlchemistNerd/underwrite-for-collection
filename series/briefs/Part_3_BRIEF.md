# Part 3 Brief

## Working title

**Regulatory Orchestration, Fair Intervention and Collection Governance**

## Scale

- Full-source target: 4,872 words.
- Editorial-prose target: 4,781 words.

## Purpose

Part 3 converts risk evidence into lawful, proportionate and testable action throughout the MSME credit lifecycle.

## Governing question

How can banks intervene early enough to improve sustainable collection while preserving affordability, privacy, explanation, dignity, contestability and human judgement?

## Core sections

1. The distinction among model inference, credit policy, customer treatment and experiment assignment.
2. Action taxonomy from reminders and payment alignment to hardship relief, restructuring and recovery.
3. Credit Policy and Compliance Gate inputs, rules, outputs and audit trail.
4. Kenyan banking, digital-credit, data-protection and consumer-protection obligations.
5. Significant automated decisions, human review and adverse-action explanations.
6. Fairness, vulnerability, contact conduct and complaint outcomes.
7. Randomised intervention governance and quasi-experimental alternatives.
8. Portfolio triggers, escalation and management information.

## Intervention design table

Each intervention will specify:

- target state;
- economic and behavioural mechanism;
- authority and owner;
- eligibility and exclusions;
- message and channel;
- expected benefit and possible harm;
- primary estimand and outcome horizon;
- monitoring and stop rule;
- human-review and complaint route;
- accounting and operational consequences.

## Tone

The manuscript will show how controls enable responsible innovation. It will integrate qualifications into the architecture rather than using adversarial or defensive language.

## Acceptance tests

- Law, supervisory guidance, contract, internal policy and statistical convention are separately labelled.
- Borrower intent is not inferred from behavioural patterns.
- Fairness is measured through multiple outcomes, not one selection-rate heuristic.
- Collection effectiveness includes durable cure, cost, complaints and business continuity.
- Randomisation never overrides mandatory support or customer-protection controls.
