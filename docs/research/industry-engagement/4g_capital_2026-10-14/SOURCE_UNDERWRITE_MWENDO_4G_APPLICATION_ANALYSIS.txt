2. What transfers from Mwendo Pamoja?
The operating mechanism changes from a vehicle generating fares to a business converting inventory into cash:
Loan → stock purchase → sales → settled customer receipts → operating needs → repayment → next stock cycle.
Our existing work	Application to 4G Capital
Mwendo’s explicit liquidity features	Measure whether cash available over the repayment horizon covers loan dues, supplier payments and essential business and household needs.
Hierarchical Bayesian underwriting	Estimate risk across business types, locations, branches, products and lending cycles, with partial pooling for groups containing limited observations.
Bayesian limit decisions	Present the expected outcome, credible interval and probability of exceeding an approved risk threshold when considering a repeat-loan increase.
Temporal and survival models	Follow when repayment, arrears, cure and recovery occur; distinguish a short delay from persistent deterioration.
Intervention framework	Evaluate the timing and effectiveness of business support, reminders, field visits and authorised repayment adjustments.
Enterprise evidence and governance	Connect field observations, mobile-money settlements, model versions, officer approvals and subsequent outcomes.
Pricing and funding analysis	Reconcile customer charges with funding, servicing, expected losses, recovery costs and the return required on capital.


For example, a trader’s timely repayment provides useful evidence for the next lending decision. Our framework adds the underlying business story: how the stock sold, what margin it generated, which receipts funded repayment and how much working capital remained afterwards.

3. KUZA is a particularly useful practical case
Direct payment to a distributor provides a starting record for the financed purchase. The research could connect that payment to:
1. The order and confirmed delivery.
2. Inventory quantities, margins and expected selling time.
3. Actual sales and cash receipts.
4. Repayment and remaining business liquidity.
5. The next lending decision.
This would make a strong entity-and-transaction-level credit case: the distributor, merchant, stock order, financing facility and payment events become linked evidence.
For underwriting, it is financing the merchant’s operating business. A subsequent financing structure could separately identify eligible lender receivables and dedicate their collections to funders. Those are two connected levels of analysis.
4. Its training model is a natural application for causal research
Because business training is part of 4G Capital’s offering, our experimental-design work has an immediate research question:
Which support, delivered to which business and at what point in its borrowing cycle, improves both business cash generation and sustainable repayment?

A practical study could compare approved forms or timings of additional support while maintaining the standard service for every participating customer.
Measure:
- Stock turnover and gross margin.
- Cash available after business and household needs.
- On-time repayment and durable cure.
- Customer experience and complaints.
- Incremental servicing cost and net collections.
A contextual bandit could later help allocate approved support as evidence accumulates. Sequential models become relevant when today’s intervention affects later business performance and lending decisions.

A reproducible framework for assessing whether each lending cycle leaves the business better able to operate and repay—and identifying which support makes that outcome more likely.
