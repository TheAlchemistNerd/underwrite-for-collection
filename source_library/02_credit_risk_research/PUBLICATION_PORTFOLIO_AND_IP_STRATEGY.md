Revised IP position
The earlier IP note needs these conceptual corrections.
Public disclosure and patent rights
Kenyan law does not say that every founder disclosure immediately and irreversibly destroys Kenyan novelty. Section 23(4) of the Industrial Property Act provides a limited 12-month exclusion for certain disclosures caused by the applicant or predecessor in title. However, mathematical methods and business methods are excluded from patentable inventions under section 21(3). Kenya Industrial Property Act.
The safe international rule remains:
Conduct invention review and file any appropriate patent application before LinkedIn, conference, preprint, GitHub or journal disclosure.

Europe has no comparable general inventor grace period; its non-prejudicial disclosures are narrowly limited mainly to evident abuse and specified exhibitions. EPO 2026 guidance.
Therefore, Kenya’s 12-month provision should be treated as a possible domestic safety net, not the publication strategy.
Copyright
Copyright automatically protects the manuscripts, original diagrams and source code as expression. It does not protect the underlying formulas, architectural ideas, business method or statistical concepts. WIPO copyright guidance.
KECOBO registration can create useful evidence of ownership and date, but it does not convert an unprotectable idea into proprietary architecture. The National Rights Registry accepts literary works, computer programs and databases, and its certificate serves as evidence of ownership. Kenya National Rights Registry.
Trade secrets
Trade-secret protection applies only while information remains secret and reasonable protection measures are maintained. Publication is incompatible with secrecy for the disclosed material. WIPO trade-secret guidance.
The following should remain outside public manuscripts and repositories:
- Production source code.
- Model weights and institution-calibrated parameters.
- Exact fraud and eligibility rules.
- Partner-specific API schemas.
- Security and key-management details.
- Proprietary data transformations.
- Customer and partner data.
- Negotiated pricing, covenants and unpublished term sheets.
- Internal validation thresholds.
- Production infrastructure credentials and configurations.
The research papers can publish the conceptual method, notation, synthetic experiment and governance logic without publishing these operational assets.
Repository and licensing
The repository currently has no LICENSE file. That leaves copyright reserved by default, but it does not create confidentiality if the repository is public.
Before any public GitHub release:
1. Confirm whether the remote is private.
2. Remove confidential transaction material.
3. Split the public research release from the private implementation repository.
4. Add an explicit manuscript licence and a separate software licence.
5. Preserve third-party notices.
6. Archive intentional research releases through Zenodo.
Zenodo assigns a DOI but requires a licence and defaults to CC BY, so that default should not be accepted without an affirmative decision. Once published, the record becomes part of a durable scholarly record. Zenodo guidance, licensing guidance.
Similarly, arXiv is permanent, may require endorsement, and requires an irrevocable distribution licence. It should be used only for focused mathematical papers after IP and journal-policy clearance. arXiv submission rules, arXiv licences.
