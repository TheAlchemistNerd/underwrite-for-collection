# Editorial enquiry to the KBA Research Centre

**To:** research@kba.co.ke  
**Subject:** Research enquiry: KESONIA, MSME credit quality and lifecycle underwriting

Dear Director, KBA Centre for Research on Financial Markets and Policy,

I am developing a Kenyan banking research paper provisionally titled **“From KESONIA to Sustainable Collection: Risk-Based Pricing, MSME Credit Quality and Lifecycle Underwriting in Kenya.”** The study examines how KESONIA-anchored risk-based pricing is transmitted into bank-product pricing and how post-disbursement information can connect underwriting, early intervention, durable cure, net recovery, IFRS 9, capital, and funding decisions.

The work is closely aligned with the 2026 conference subtheme on monetary policy and bank credit-pricing dynamics, including credit access, NPL management, and credit de-risking. It combines a proposed public-data analysis of KESONIA, CBK lending rates, KBA Total Cost of Credit data, the KBA MSME Credit Dashboard, and Credit Officer Surveys with an institution-level lifecycle study, subject to securing anonymised account data.

I appreciate that the published 2026 proposal, full-paper, and technical-review deadlines have passed. Could you kindly advise whether the Centre would consider this work for:

- a late methodological-session contribution, if any space remains;
- off-cycle review for the KBA Working Paper Series;
- a shorter article for the Kenya Bankers Economic Bulletin; or
- the next Annual Banking Research Conference cycle?

I can provide a one-page concept note or the five-page research proposal immediately.
